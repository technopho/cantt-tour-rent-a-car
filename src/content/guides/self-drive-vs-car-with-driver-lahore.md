Both options get you a clean car. The difference is who drives, who deals with Lahore traffic and parking, and who carries the responsibility on the road. Here is how to decide.

## The quick answer

**Choose a car with a driver if:**

- You do not know Lahore well, or it has been years since you drove here
- You are going to a wedding, a hospital, the airport or a series of meetings
- You are heading north to the mountains
- You want to work, rest or look after family during the ride

**Choose self drive if:**

- You are a confident Lahore driver with a valid licence
- You want privacy and full control of your schedule
- You mostly need the car for errands, short trips and evenings out
- You are renting weekly or monthly and want the lowest daily cost

## What each option includes

### Car with a driver

- The car and a professional driver for the standard duty hours of each day
- Waiting time while you shop, meet or visit
- Parking, directions and dealing with traffic wardens
- Fuel, tolls and parking at actual cost, plus overtime beyond duty hours and a night allowance on outstation trips

### Self drive

- The car for 24-hour days, with a daily km allowance
- A refundable security deposit at handover
- You drive, park and refuel. Fuel is on you
- You are responsible for traffic fines and any damage, as written in the agreement

## Cost compared

Per day, self drive is cheaper because you are not paying for the driver's time. But the real comparison is the **total cost of your trip**:

- **Self drive** adds the deposit (refundable), fuel, parking and the risk of fines or damage.
- **With driver** adds the driver's overtime on long days and night allowances outstation, but removes the deposit and most of the risk.

For a quiet week of errands, self drive usually wins. For a wedding weekend, an airport run with heavy luggage or a trip north, a driver is often better value once you count everything. The live comparison further down this page shows both daily rates for the same cars.

## Driving in Lahore: what to expect

If you are thinking of self drive, be realistic about Lahore traffic:

- **Motorbikes and rickshaws** weave through every gap. Expect the unexpected on both sides.
- **Lane discipline** is loose, and U-turns are part of the road layout on big roads like Main Boulevard and Canal Road.
- **Safe City cameras** issue e-challans for signal violations and speeding. On a rental, the fine follows the car and comes back to you.
- **Parking** in Gulberg, Liberty, Anarkali and the Walled City is hard at peak times.
- **Fog and smog** from December to January reduce visibility, especially early in the morning and on the motorways.

None of this is a problem for an experienced local driver. It can be stressful if you have just landed from abroad.

- You live in Lahore and need a car while yours is in the workshop.
- You want a car for the week to run errands at your own pace.
- You are renting monthly and are comfortable with Lahore traffic. See [monthly car rental](/monthly-car-rental-lahore/).

## Responsibility and risk

With self drive, you are responsible for the car while it is with you: fuel, fines, minor damage and following the agreement. That is why there is a deposit. With a driver, the driver is responsible for driving and the car, and you are responsible only for the agreed charges.

Either way, read the agreement, check the car together at handover and keep the receipts. See the [documents you need](/guides/documents-to-rent-a-car-in-pakistan/) before you book.

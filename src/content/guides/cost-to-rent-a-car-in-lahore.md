The honest answer is: it depends on six things, and once you understand them you can compare any two quotes in Lahore in a minute. This guide explains each one. For today's numbers, see our [rates page](/rates/), which is updated straight from our fleet, and the live tables further down this page.

## The short answer

In Lahore you pay **per day** for a car, and the daily price moves with:

1. **The car** you choose.
2. **With driver or self drive.**
3. **Where you go:** inside Lahore or outstation.
4. **How long** you keep it.
5. **When** you book: dates and demand.
6. **Extras:** fuel, tolls, parking, overtime and deposits.

Everything else is detail. Let's go through them.

## What decides the price

### 1. The car

The biggest factor is the car itself. Roughly from lowest to highest daily price:

- **Hatchbacks** such as the Suzuki Alto and Cultus: city errands, one or two people.
- **Sedans** such as the Honda City, Toyota Corolla and Honda Civic: the everyday choice for families, airport runs and business.
- **SUVs** such as the Honda BR-V, Kia Sportage and Toyota Fortuner: more space, more comfort, and the right choice for mountain roads.
- **Luxury and wedding cars** such as the Toyota Prado, Land Cruiser and Mercedes-Benz S-Class: VIP movement and weddings.
- **Vans and coasters** such as the Toyota Hiace and Coaster: groups, priced per vehicle rather than per seat.

Browse them all on the [fleet page](/cars/).

### 2. With driver or self drive

A [car with a driver](/rent-a-car-with-driver-lahore/) costs more per day than the same car on [self drive](/self-drive-car-rental-lahore/), because the price includes the driver's time. In return, there is usually no security deposit, no parking stress and no responsibility for traffic fines. Self drive is cheaper per day but needs a valid licence and a refundable deposit, and you handle everything on the road. Our [self drive vs driver guide](/guides/self-drive-vs-car-with-driver-lahore/) compares the two in detail.

### 3. Inside Lahore or outstation

A city day with a driver covers the driver's standard duty hours inside Lahore. Trips out of the city use the **outstation rate**, which works differently:

- Days are counted in **24-hour blocks** from your pickup time.
- For every night away from Lahore, a **driver night allowance** covers the driver's stay and meals.
- **Fuel, tolls and parking** are charged at actual cost.

Each [route page](/routes/) shows the outstation rate for the cars we recommend, plus a worked example.

### 4. How long you keep it

The longer you rent, the lower the effective daily price. Weekly self drive rates and [monthly car rental](/monthly-car-rental-lahore/) rates cost noticeably less per day than booking day by day. If you need a car for three weeks, always ask what a month would cost.

### 5. Dates and demand

Lahore has clear peaks: the winter **wedding season** (roughly November to February), **Eid** holidays, long weekends and summer holidays, when families head north. On those dates, the best cars go first. Book early, and check whether peak date pricing applies to your car: a good quote says so plainly.

### 6. The extras

These are where quotes really differ, so read them carefully:

- **Fuel:** usually paid by you, either by filling up or at actual cost.
- **Tolls and parking:** motorway tolls (M-Tag) and parking at actual cost.
- **Driver overtime:** hours beyond standard duty hours, charged per hour.
- **Driver night allowance:** on outstation trips, per night away.
- **Security deposit:** for self drive, refundable after the car comes back in the same condition.
- **Decoration:** for wedding cars, sometimes quoted separately.

## What is usually included, and what is not

| Usually included | Usually paid separately |
| --- | --- |
| The car, cleaned and checked | Fuel |
| The driver and his standard duty hours (driver bookings) | Motorway tolls and parking |
| Pickup from your home, office or hotel in Lahore | Driver overtime beyond duty hours |
| A daily km allowance (self drive) | Driver night allowance on outstation trips |
| | Refundable security deposit (self drive) |

## How to get the best price

- **Book early** for weddings, Eid and summer trips north.
- **Be flexible on the car.** A Toyota Yaris or Honda City does the same airport run as a Civic for less.
- **Go monthly** if you need a car for more than two or three weeks.
- **Choose self drive** for simple city use, if you are a confident Lahore driver.
- **Combine trips.** An airport pickup plus two city days is often better value as one booking.
- **Ask for a written quote** on WhatsApp, with every inclusion and exclusion listed.

## Red flags when comparing quotes

- A very low daily price with no mention of fuel, hours or kilometres.
- No written terms for the deposit: how much, and when it comes back.
- Vague answers about who pays motorway tolls, parking or traffic fines.
- Pressure to pay the full amount in advance before you have seen the car or the terms.

Johar Town sits on the south-western side of Lahore, along Canal Road, with **Emporium Mall** and **Expo Centre** at its heart and Thokar Niaz Baig just down the road. It is a mix of family homes, student hostels, hospitals and big event venues, which makes for very different car rental needs on the same street.

## Hospital visits

**Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital** and **Doctors Hospital** draw patients from all over Pakistan. Families who come to Lahore for treatment often need a reliable car for days or weeks: drop-offs at the entrance, a driver who waits through long appointments, and a calm ride back to where they are staying.

A car with a driver on a daily booking covers this. For longer stays, a [monthly car rental](/monthly-car-rental-lahore/) is usually the better deal. If you are flying in, we can start with an [airport pickup](/lahore-airport-car-rental/) and keep the same car for the rest of your stay.

## Exhibitions at Expo Centre

Expo Centre hosts trade shows, book fairs and exhibitions through the year. On big days the roads around it slow right down and parking fills early. Exhibitors usually book:

- A sedan or two with drivers for the team, on call from setup to the last evening.
- A [Toyota Hiace or a coaster](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/) for staff, samples and stand material.
- Airport pickups for visiting buyers and partners.

## Students and universities

The University of Central Punjab and other campuses nearby mean plenty of students who need a car for a weekend, a trip home or a family visit. A [self drive car](/self-drive-car-rental-lahore/) works if you meet the requirements. For trips home to another city, a car with a driver on a one way or return trip is often less tiring. See our [documents guide](/guides/documents-to-rent-a-car-in-pakistan/) for what to bring.

## A good base for road trips

Thokar Niaz Baig is about 15 minutes from most of Johar Town, which puts you on the M-2 for [Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/) and [Faisalabad](/routes/lahore-to-faisalabad/) sooner than almost anywhere else in Lahore. The M-3 to [Multan](/routes/lahore-to-multan/) is close too.

## Local tips from our drivers

- **Canal Road peaks:** Canal Road towards Gulberg and the Mall is slowest from about 8 to 10 am and 5 to 8 pm.
- **Emporium on weekends:** the roads around Emporium Mall back up on Friday and Saturday evenings. Agree a pickup entrance in advance.
- **Expo days:** if your pickup is near Expo Centre during a big exhibition, add 20 minutes to your plan.
- **Winter fog:** early mornings near Thokar Niaz Baig and the motorway can be foggy in December and January.

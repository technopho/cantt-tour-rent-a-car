Gulberg is where Lahore does business by day and eats out by night. The district runs along **Main Boulevard**, with **MM Alam Road** and **Liberty Market** on one side and Gaddafi Stadium and Ferozepur Road on the other. Almost everything is within a few kilometres, but finding a parking spot can take longer than the drive.

## Why a driver makes sense in Gulberg

Parking is the real problem here. MM Alam Road's restaurants rely on valets and roadside spaces, Liberty Market's car park fills up on weekend evenings, and traffic wardens ticket double parking quickly. A car with a driver turns that into a door-to-door service: you step out at the entrance, the driver finds a spot, and comes back when you call.

It also helps with the one-way loops and U-turns along Main Boulevard, which can turn a 500 metre trip into a 3 kilometre one if you miss the turn.

## Evenings out and events

Dinner on MM Alam Road, shopping at Liberty or a match at Gaddafi Stadium: evenings are when Gulberg is busiest. On match days, roads around the stadium close and security cordons go up hours before the start, so we drop you at the nearest open point and agree a pickup spot away from the crowd.

## Local tips from our drivers

- **Evening peak:** Main Boulevard and the roads around Kalma Chowk are slowest from about 5 to 8 pm on weekdays.
- **Liberty on weekends:** after 8 pm on Fridays and Saturdays, expect queues for the car park and around the roundabout.
- **Heading to Islamabad:** from Gulberg, Canal Road to Thokar Niaz Baig is usually the quickest way onto the M-2. See [Lahore to Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/).
- **Hafeez Centre:** parking is hard all day. A short wait with a driver beats circling the block.

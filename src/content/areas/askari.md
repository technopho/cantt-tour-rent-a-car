Askari housing schemes are gated communities originally built for armed forces families, and several of them sit on the Cantt side of Lahore. **Askari 10** is right next to Allama Iqbal International Airport, **Askari 11** is further out towards Bedian Road, and the older sectors sit inside Lahore Cantt itself. Being close to our office means quick pickups and easy handovers.

## Gate entry, done right

Every Askari sector has guarded gates. Visitors give the house number at the barrier, and some sectors check with the resident by phone before letting a car in. To keep it smooth:

- Share the **sector, street and house number** when you book, not just the sector.
- If your sector issues visitor passes or keeps a list, add the driver's details when we send them.
- Keep your phone nearby at pickup time in case the guard calls.

## Airport runs from Askari 10

From Askari 10 the airport is about 10 minutes away in normal traffic. That makes it one of the easiest places in Lahore for a 4 am flight or a late night arrival. Book an [airport pick and drop](/lahore-airport-car-rental/) and share your flight number.

## Families on posting and new arrivals

Families moving to Lahore often need a car from day one: school admissions, house hunting, shopping and settling in. A [monthly car rental](/monthly-car-rental-lahore/) is the practical answer until your own car arrives. With a driver, you also get someone who knows the city while you learn it.

For weekend trips, Askari is well placed: the Ring Road leads to the M-2 for [Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/), and the [Wagah Border](/routes/lahore-to-wagah-border/) is about 35 minutes away. For summer trips north, an SUV for [Skardu](/routes/lahore-to-skardu/) or Hunza is worth booking early.

- **Airport Road peaks:** Airport Road and the roads into Cantt slow down at school and office times.
- **Ring Road access:** for the M-2, DHA and Wagah, the Ring Road is usually the quickest option from Askari 10.

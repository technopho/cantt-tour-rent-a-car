Model Town is one of Lahore's oldest planned neighbourhoods, laid out around a large central park with tree-lined blocks in rings around it. It is quiet, residential and full of families, with **Ferozepur Road** on its eastern edge and **Link Road** connecting it to Faisal Town and beyond.

## Family errands and hospital visits

Most Model Town requests are about family: elderly parents going to the doctor, a day of bank visits and shopping, or relatives arriving from abroad. A car with a driver on a [daily booking](/rent-a-car-with-driver-lahore/) means no parking stress at busy clinics, a driver who waits, and a safe ride home.

If relatives are flying in, start with an [airport pickup](/lahore-airport-car-rental/). Allama Iqbal International Airport is about 35 minutes away in normal traffic.

## Weddings and family functions

Model Town families often host functions at home and in nearby marquees. For a wedding, think in three layers:

1. **The couple's car:** a decorated sedan or executive car. See [wedding car rental](/wedding-car-rental-lahore/).
2. **Close family:** two or three matching sedans or SUVs.
3. **Guests and elders:** a [Toyota Hiace or a coaster](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/) that does the rounds between homes, the venue and guest houses.

## Getting out of the city

From Model Town, Canal Road leads to Thokar Niaz Baig and the M-2 in about 25 minutes, the Ring Road takes you east to the airport and Wagah, and Ferozepur Road runs south out of the city. Day trips to [Kartarpur and Nankana Sahib](/routes/lahore-to-kartarpur-nankana-sahib/) are popular, and so is a weekend in [Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/).

## Local tips from our drivers

- **Morning walkers:** Model Town Park is busy early in the morning. Pickups at the park gates are easy, but the inner roads around it are slow between 6 and 8 am.
- **Ferozepur Road peaks:** the stretch past Kalma Chowk is slowest from about 8 to 10 am and 5 to 8 pm. The underpass helps, but plan for delays.
- **School times:** many schools in and around Model Town mean slow streets at drop-off and pickup times.
- **Finding the house:** the blocks are easy once you know them, but visitors get lost. A location pin on WhatsApp gets the driver to your gate first time.

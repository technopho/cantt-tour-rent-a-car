Wapda Town is a settled residential area on the south-west side of Lahore, built around a busy **main roundabout** with markets, food spots and everyday shops. It sits next to Johar Town and close to Valencia, and it is one of the quickest parts of the city to reach the motorway from.

## A good starting point for road trips

From Wapda Town, Thokar Niaz Baig is about 15 minutes away, which puts you on the M-2 well before anyone leaving from Cantt or DHA. Trips to [Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/) and the north get going quickly from here, and the M-3 makes [Multan](/routes/lahore-to-multan/) an easy run too.

For long trips, read our [northern areas road trip guide](/guides/lahore-to-northern-areas-road-trip-guide/) before you pick a car.

## Getting into the city

The trade-off for being close to the motorway is distance from central Lahore. Canal Road leads to Gulberg and the Mall, and the Ring Road loops east to DHA and the airport. In normal traffic, Kalma Chowk is about 30 minutes away and the airport about 50. A car with a driver turns that time into something useful.

## Family functions and weddings

Many families in Wapda Town and Valencia hold functions at home and in nearby halls. For a wedding, book a decorated car for the couple through our [wedding car rental](/wedding-car-rental-lahore/), plus a [van or coaster](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/) for relatives coming from other cities.

## Local tips from our drivers

- **The roundabout in the evening:** the main roundabout and its markets get crowded after Maghrib, especially on weekends. Agree a pickup point a short walk away.
- **School times:** school drop-off and pickup slow down the main roads in the morning and early afternoon.
- **Fog near Thokar:** winter mornings can be foggy around Thokar Niaz Baig and the motorway. Early starts in December and January need extra time.

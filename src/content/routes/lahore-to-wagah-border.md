The daily flag lowering ceremony at the Wagah Border is one of Lahore's most memorable evenings: crowds cheering in the stands, Rangers in full uniform marching high-stepped to the gate, and the flags coming down at sunset. It is a short drive from the city, but timing matters.

## The drive

From central Lahore, the driver heads east along the **GT Road**, past **Shalimar Gardens** and through Batapur to the border, about 25 km in total. From Lahore Cantt and Askari it is a little closer, and the Ring Road can save time from DHA.

In normal traffic it takes 40 to 50 minutes, but the last few kilometres slow down in the hour before the ceremony as cars, buses and coaches arrive together.

## Timing your visit

- **The ceremony starts shortly before sunset**, so the time shifts with the seasons. Check the sunset time for your date.
- **Arrive early:** aim to reach the parking area at least an hour and a half before sunset. Security checks and the walk to the stands take time, and good seats fill up.
- **Getting out:** after the ceremony, thousands of people leave at once. The driver waits at an agreed spot so you do not wander the car park looking for the car.

## What to carry

- **CNIC** for Pakistani visitors, **passport** for foreign visitors.
- **Phone and wallet only.** Bags are usually not allowed through security, so leave them in the car.
- **Water**, especially in summer, and a light jacket in December and January.

## Make an evening of it

Combine the ceremony with a stop at **Shalimar Gardens** on the way, the Mughal garden on the GT Road, or dinner in the city on the way back. Families with children sometimes add Jallo Park, which is also on the eastern side of Lahore.

Two of the holiest places in Sikhism are within a day's drive of Lahore. **Gurdwara Janam Asthan** in Nankana Sahib marks the birthplace of Guru Nanak, and **Gurdwara Darbar Sahib** in Kartarpur is where he spent his final years. Visitors from Pakistan and abroad travel to both, and a car with a driver makes the trips calm and simple.

## Nankana Sahib

Nankana Sahib is about 85 km west of Lahore. The driver heads out past **Sheikhupura** and on to Nankana, around 2 hours in normal traffic. Besides Gurdwara Janam Asthan, the town has several other historic gurdwaras connected to Guru Nanak's early life, all within a short drive of each other.

## Kartarpur

Kartarpur is north-east of Lahore in Narowal district, a few kilometres from the border. The usual route runs through **Narowal**, about 125 km and 2.5 to 3 hours in total. Gurdwara Darbar Sahib sits in a large, well-kept complex, and visitors usually spend a few hours there.

## One day or two?

Because the two are in opposite directions from Lahore, there are two ways to plan:

- **Two day trips:** one day for Nankana Sahib and one for Kartarpur. Relaxed, with time to stay for prayers and langar.
- **One long day:** leave Lahore very early for Nankana Sahib, come back past Lahore and continue to Kartarpur. It works, but expect a late return.

Our [city day bookings with a driver](/rent-a-car-with-driver-lahore/) cover standard duty hours. A long day that runs beyond them uses the overtime rate shown for each car.

## Festival dates

Large gatherings take place at Nankana Sahib for **Guru Nanak's birthday (Gurpurab)**, usually in November, and at other Sikh festivals through the year. On those days, security is tight and roads near the gurdwaras are crowded. Book your car early, leave earlier than usual and agree a pickup point with the driver.

## Documents and etiquette

- Carry your **CNIC** (Pakistani visitors) or **passport with a valid visa** (foreign visitors).
- Entry arrangements and any fees are set by the authorities that run the gurdwaras and can change. Check before you go.
- Cover your head inside the gurdwaras and remove your shoes. Head coverings are usually available at the entrance.

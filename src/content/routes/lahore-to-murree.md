Murree is the hill station Lahore escapes to, in summer for the cool air and in winter for the snow. With a driver, it is an easy family trip: motorway most of the way, then an hour of pine forest and hairpin bends.

## The drive

The first four to five hours are the same as [Lahore to Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/) on the M-2. Near Islamabad, the driver takes the **Murree Expressway**, which climbs steadily through the Margalla foothills to Murree. The air cools quickly, and in winter you will start seeing snow on the slopes well before town.

## What to see

- **Mall Road** is the heart of Murree, with shops, food stalls and evening crowds.
- **Patriata (New Murree)** has a chairlift and cable car with views over the hills.
- **Nathia Gali and Ayubia** in the Galiyat are quieter and greener, with walking trails and pine forest.
- **Bhurban** has a golf course and wide views over the valleys.

## Snow trips: what to know

Snow in Murree usually falls between December and February. It is the busiest and most delicate time to visit:

- **Pick an SUV.** Ground clearance and grip matter on icy roads. A Toyota Fortuner or Kia Sportage handles it far better than a small sedan.
- **Follow the advisories.** In heavy snow, the authorities limit how many cars can enter Murree and close roads if they become unsafe.
- **Stay flexible.** Do not plan a tight schedule. Leave daylight margin for the drive back down.
- **Pack properly:** warm layers, gloves, snacks, water, a charged power bank and medicines.

## Summer and holidays

From May to August and over Eid holidays, Murree is busy. Traffic on the Expressway and Mall Road can crawl in the evenings. Leave Lahore early, arrive by afternoon, and do your sightseeing in the morning.

## Choosing a car

For a family of four in summer, a Honda BR-V or Kia Sportage is comfortable and copes well with the climb. In snow season, the Toyota Fortuner is the safest choice. Bigger families and friends' groups travel together in a [Toyota Hiace](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/).

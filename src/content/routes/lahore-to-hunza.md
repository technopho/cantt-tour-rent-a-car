Hunza is the trip people talk about for years: Rakaposhi glowing at sunset, the turquoise water of Attabad Lake, the Passu Cones and the long climb to Khunjerab on the Chinese border. From Lahore it is a serious road trip, and a driver who knows the Karakoram Highway makes all the difference.

## Two ways to get there

Both routes start with the M-2 from Lahore to Islamabad and the Hazara Motorway towards Mansehra.

- **Summer route via Babusar Pass (roughly June to October):** through the Kaghan valley to [Naran](/routes/lahore-to-naran-kaghan/), over Babusar Top and down to **Chilas**, where you join the Karakoram Highway. Shorter, more scenic, and a natural overnight stop in Naran.
- **Karakoram Highway route (most of the year):** from Mansehra along the N-35 through Thakot, **Besham**, Dasu and Chilas. Longer and slower in places, with construction zones and landslide-prone stretches, but open when Babusar is snowed in.

From Chilas, both routes follow the Indus past Raikot Bridge (the jeep base for Fairy Meadows) and the **junction of the Hindu Kush, Karakoram and Himalaya ranges** near Jaglot, then on to **Gilgit** and up the Hunza valley to Aliabad and Karimabad.

## What to see in Hunza

- **Karimabad:** Baltit Fort, Altit Fort and the old bazaar, with Rakaposhi and Ultar peaks in view.
- **Rakaposhi viewpoint:** a roadside stop in Nagar with a close view of the mountain.
- **Attabad Lake:** the turquoise lake formed by a landslide in 2010, with tunnels along its edge.
- **Passu Cones and the Hussaini suspension bridge** in Gojal, Upper Hunza.
- **Khunjerab Pass:** the border with China, above 4,600 metres. Open seasonally.

## Planning and safety

- **Daylight driving only** on the Karakoram Highway and Babusar road.
- **Buffer days:** landslides and rockfalls can close the highway for hours or a day. Keep one or two spare days.
- **Altitude:** Khunjerab and Babusar are high. Go up slowly and come down if anyone gets a headache or feels sick.
- **Fuel and cash:** fill up in Chilas and Gilgit. Carry enough cash, as ATMs and card machines are limited.
- **Documents:** carry CNIC for Pakistanis and passport with visa for foreign visitors. There are police check posts along the way.
- **Phone signal:** coverage is patchy between valleys. Share your plan with family before you leave.

## Choosing a car

For Hunza we recommend a **Toyota Fortuner or Prado** with a driver who has done the Karakoram Highway before. For groups of eight or more, a [Toyota Hiace or coaster](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/) keeps everyone together. ## Combine with Skardu

With a few extra days, you can combine Hunza with [Skardu](/routes/lahore-to-skardu/): the Skardu road branches off the Karakoram Highway at Jaglot, between Chilas and Gilgit.

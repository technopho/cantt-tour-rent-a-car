Faisalabad is Pakistan's textile capital and the third largest city in the country. Most trips from Lahore are for business: mills, buying houses and the busy markets around the Clock Tower. With the motorway, it is an easy day out.

## The drive

The driver takes the **M-2** north-west from Lahore, past Sheikhupura and the Sukheki service area, to **Pindi Bhattian**. There the **M-4** branches off south-west and runs straight to Faisalabad. The motorways are quick and quiet compared with the old road through Sheikhupura.

## Inside Faisalabad

Faisalabad's centre is laid out around the **Clock Tower (Ghanta Ghar)**, with eight bazaars running out from it like the spokes of a wheel, including the famous cloth markets. Traffic in the bazaars is slow and parking is scarce, so a car with a driver who drops you at the entrance saves a lot of time.

## Who books this trip

- **Textile and garment buyers** visiting mills and suppliers, often with a same day return.
- **Families** going to weddings and visiting relatives.
- **Students and parents** travelling to and from universities.
- **Travellers** heading for flights from Faisalabad.

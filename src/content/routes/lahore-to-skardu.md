Skardu is the gateway to Baltistan: wide valleys between huge mountains, cold desert dunes, blue lakes and old forts. It is also one of the longest road trips you can take from Lahore, and one of the most rewarding.

## The drive

The first day follows the northern routes out of Lahore: the M-2 to Islamabad and the Hazara Motorway towards Mansehra. In summer, most travellers cross **Babusar Pass** from [Naran](/routes/lahore-to-naran-kaghan/) to **Chilas**. When Babusar is closed, the Karakoram Highway through Besham and Dasu reaches Chilas instead.

From Chilas, the Karakoram Highway follows the Indus to **Jaglot**, where you can stop at the viewpoint for the **junction of the Hindu Kush, Karakoram and Himalaya ranges**. Here the **Jaglot to Skardu road** turns east and follows the Indus through a deep gorge for most of the way to Skardu. It is one of the most dramatic drives in Pakistan.

## What to see around Skardu

- **Shangrila and Lower Kachura Lake:** the famous resort lake a short drive from town.
- **Upper Kachura Lake:** clear, deep and quieter, with a short walk down from the road.
- **Shigar Fort:** a restored fort and village in the Shigar valley.
- **Khaplu:** a quiet valley town with its own palace and mosque, a few hours east of Skardu.
- **Sarfaranga cold desert:** sand dunes in the middle of the mountains.
- **Deosai Plains:** high alpine plateau, usually accessible from about July to September.
- **Manthokha waterfall:** a popular day trip with a roadside stop.

## Planning and safety

- **Daylight only** on the Babusar road, the Karakoram Highway and the Indus gorge road.
- **Spare days:** rockfalls and landslides can stop traffic for a few hours or longer. Keep one or two buffer days.
- **Altitude:** Babusar and Deosai are high. Take it slowly and keep water, snacks and medicines handy.
- **Cash and fuel:** fill up in Chilas and Skardu, and carry enough cash for the trip.
- **Documents:** CNIC for Pakistanis, passport with visa for foreigners. Expect police check posts.

## Choosing a car

A **Toyota Fortuner or Prado** is the right car for Skardu: ground clearance, strong brakes for long descents and comfort on two long driving days. For groups, a [Toyota Hiace](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/) works well on the main roads. For Deosai and rough side tracks, local jeeps are often the better tool. Read our [northern areas road trip guide](/guides/lahore-to-northern-areas-road-trip-guide/) before you plan dates.

## Combine with Hunza

Skardu and [Hunza](/routes/lahore-to-hunza/) share the route as far as Jaglot. With ten to twelve days, you can see both without rushing.

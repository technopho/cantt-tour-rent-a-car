The Kaghan valley is the classic summer road trip from Lahore: green slopes, the roaring Kunhar river, glacier-fed lakes and cool nights in July. Naran is the base, and with a driver who knows the mountain road, the long drive becomes part of the holiday.

## The drive

From Lahore, the M-2 takes you towards Islamabad. Just before the city, the driver switches to the M-1 and then the **Hazara Motorway**, which runs north past Havelian and Abbottabad to **Mansehra**. From Mansehra, the N-15 drops into the Kaghan valley at **Balakot** and follows the Kunhar river up through **Kiwai**, **Kaghan** village and on to **Naran**.

The motorway part is fast. The valley road is slower: allow three to four hours from Balakot to Naran, more on weekends or after rain.

## What to see around Naran

- **Lake Saiful Muluk:** the famous lake above Naran, reached by local jeep.
- **Lulusar Lake:** a long, quiet lake on the way to Babusar Top.
- **Babusar Top:** the high pass at the top of the valley, open roughly June to October, with wide views on a clear day.
- **Batakundi and Lalazar:** green meadows above Naran, a short drive or jeep ride away.
- **Shogran:** a hill top meadow above Kiwai, reached by jeep from the valley road.

## Safety in the valley

- **Daylight only on the mountain road.** We plan departures so the Balakot to Naran stretch is done before dark.
- **Monsoon caution:** in July and August, heavy rain can trigger landslides. The driver checks conditions each morning and you keep a spare day in the plan.
- **Cold at night:** even in July, Naran evenings are cool and Babusar Top can be near freezing. Pack warm layers.
- **Cash and fuel:** fill up in Balakot or Kaghan, and carry cash because card machines and ATMs are few.

## Choosing a car

A **Toyota Fortuner or Prado** is the best choice for the valley and essential if you plan to cross Babusar. For a summer trip that stays in Naran, a Honda BR-V is comfortable for a family. Groups travel well in a [Toyota Hiace or a coaster](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/). Our [northern areas guide](/guides/lahore-to-northern-areas-road-trip-guide/) covers seasons, packing and safety in more detail.

## Going further

When Babusar is open, Naran is the summer gateway to Gilgit-Baltistan. Cross the pass to Chilas and continue to [Hunza](/routes/lahore-to-hunza/) or [Skardu](/routes/lahore-to-skardu/) for the trip of a lifetime.

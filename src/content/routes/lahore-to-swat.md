Swat is the green valley north of Peshawar: river views, orchards, the ski slopes of Malam Jabba and the high meadows around Kalam. The Swat Expressway has made it a much easier trip from Lahore than it used to be.

## The drive

From Lahore, the M-2 takes you towards Islamabad. Before the city, the driver joins the **M-1** towards Peshawar, then turns north onto the **Swat Expressway**, which crosses the Malakand hills to **Chakdara**. From there it is a short drive up the Swat river to **Mingora** and **Saidu Sharif**, the main towns of the valley.

## What to see

- **Fizagat:** a riverside park just outside Mingora, popular with families.
- **Malam Jabba:** the ski resort above the valley, with a chairlift and snow in winter.
- **Bahrain:** a riverside town halfway up the valley, a good lunch stop.
- **Kalam:** the upper valley, with forests, the Ushu valley and Mahodand Lake (by jeep).
- **The Swat Museum and Buddhist sites** around Saidu Sharif, for history lovers.

## Seasons and safety

- **Summer (May to September):** the best time for Kalam and the upper valley. Expect weekend crowds.
- **Monsoon (July and August):** heavy rain can bring flash floods on the Swat river. Stay away from the river bank in storms and keep a spare day.
- **Winter (December to February):** snow at Malam Jabba. The upper valley road can close.
- **Daylight driving** above Mingora, especially on the road to Kalam.

## Choosing a car

For Mingora and Malam Jabba in good weather, a Honda BR-V or Kia Sportage is comfortable for a family. For Kalam or snow, pick a **Toyota Fortuner**. Groups and extended families travel together in a [Toyota Hiace](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/). See the [northern areas guide](/guides/lahore-to-northern-areas-road-trip-guide/) for packing and safety tips.

## Combine with Islamabad

Swat pairs well with a night in [Islamabad](/routes/lahore-to-islamabad/) on the way up or back, which keeps each driving day shorter.

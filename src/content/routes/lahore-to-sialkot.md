Sialkot is one of Pakistan's busiest export cities, known worldwide for sports goods, surgical instruments and leather. With the M-11 motorway, it is now an easy day trip from Lahore for business visits, factory audits, family events and flights from Sialkot International Airport.

## The drive

The driver takes the **Lahore Ring Road** north to Kala Shah Kaku, where the **M-11 Lahore to Sialkot Motorway** begins. It is a straight, quick run past the Gujranwala and Daska interchanges to **Sambrial**, home of Sialkot International Airport, and on into Sialkot.

## Who books this trip

- **Business visitors** heading to factories and export houses, often with a same day return.
- **Buyers and auditors** flying into Lahore who need a car and driver for a few days of visits in Sialkot, Gujranwala and Wazirabad.
- **Families** going to weddings and visiting relatives.
- **Travellers** catching flights from Sialkot International Airport.

## Same day return or overnight

A same day return is easy: about four hours of driving in total. If your schedule has meetings in several cities, an overnight stay with the driver's night allowance gives you more flexibility. For a full week of visits, ask about a weekly arrangement.

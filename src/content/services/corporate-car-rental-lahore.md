## Who uses a corporate account

- **Offices in Gulberg, DHA and on The Mall.** Banks, multinationals, software houses and consultancies with visitors to collect, managers who need a car, and meetings across town.
- **Factories and industrial estates.** Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate on Kot Lakhpat, Sundar Industrial Estate on Raiwind Road, and the units along Multan Road and Ferozepur Road. Foreign buyers and auditors need reliable cars from the airport and the hotel.
- **Project teams and NGOs.** Field visits to Kasur, Sheikhupura and Okara, or a team based in Lahore for a few months.
- **Event organisers.** Exhibitions at the Expo Centre in Johar Town, conferences at hotels on The Mall and in Gulberg, and corporate dinners.

## How the account works

1. **A short call or email.** Tell us the trip patterns: daily, weekly, seasonal, events. We suggest the cars and the cheapest way to cover them, by the day or by the month.
2. **A written rate sheet.** Rates per car, duty hours, overtime, fuel arrangement and outstation terms, signed by both sides.
3. **Bookers and approvals.** Your list of authorised bookers, and any approval rules, for example a manager must approve outstation trips.
4. **Booking.** Bookers message us on WhatsApp or email. We confirm with a reference number, the driver's name and number and the car registration.
5. **Monthly invoice.** One invoice with every trip listed by reference, date and booker, plus a statement of payments.

## Billing, NTN and paperwork

Invoices are issued in your company's name, with your NTN and billing address, and purchase order numbers where your finance team uses them. Trip logs with times and routes are available on request. We work with your finance department's requirements on payment terms and tax documents; tell us at setup what they need so the first invoice is right.

## Executive cars and visiting guests

Senior visitors expect a car that is spotless and a driver who is on time and discreet. We meet arrivals at Allama Iqbal International Airport with a name board, take them to hotels on The Mall or in Gulberg, and stay with them for meetings across the city. ## Staff transport

Vans and coasters run fixed routes at fixed times, for shift starts at a factory or the morning office run. We plan the route with the fewest stops, share the driver's number with your admin team, and keep a standby vehicle in mind for breakdowns. ## Self drive pool cars

For managers who prefer to drive themselves, a pool car on a monthly contract works out cheaper than reimbursing private cars. Every employee who drives is registered with their CNIC and licence. Traffic challans during the month are billed to the company with a copy of each challan, so you can recover them internally if you choose.

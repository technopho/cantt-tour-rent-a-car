## Who self drive suits

- **People who know Lahore and want privacy.** Family errands, a weekend with the in-laws, late evenings without a driver waiting outside.
- **Overseas Pakistanis home for a few weeks.** Keep one car for the whole stay at a weekly or monthly price, and drive to family in Sialkot or Gujranwala when you need to.
- **Your own car is in the workshop.** A stand-in for a few days after an accident or during a long repair.
- **Professionals between cars.** Waiting for a new car delivery, or just transferred to Lahore and not ready to buy.

If you are not confident in Lahore traffic, or you are visiting from abroad on a foreign licence, a [car with driver](/rent-a-car-with-driver-lahore/) is often the easier choice, and not much more expensive for a short stay.

## Verify first, drive sooner

Send clear photos of your CNIC and driving licence on WhatsApp when you book. We check them before the day, so handover takes minutes instead of an hour. Bring the originals with you: we look at them at handover and return them straight away.

## Handover in Lahore Cantt

We hand over self drive cars at our office in Lahore Cantt, or at your address in Lahore on request. At handover we:

1. Walk around the car with you and take photos or a short video of every panel, the wheels and the interior.
2. Note the fuel level and the odometer reading on the agreement.
3. Show you the spare tyre, the jack and the papers in the glovebox.
4. Collect the refundable deposit and both sign the agreement.

The same check happens in reverse when you return the car. Because both sides recorded the condition at the start, there is nothing to argue about at the end.

- **Kilometres.** Each car has a daily allowance, listed below. Long trips use it up quickly: a round trip from Lahore Cantt to Islamabad is around 750 km.

- **What the car is not for.** Ride hailing or delivery work, driving lessons, racing, towing, or carrying goods for business.

## Safe City cameras and e-challans

Lahore's Safe City cameras record signal and lane violations at many junctions, and speed on some roads. An e-challan is issued to the car's registration, not to you, and it can take days to appear. That is why we check the e-challan record after every self drive return before we refund the deposit.

A few habits keep you clear: stop behind the white line at signals, do not cross into the opposite lane to skip a queue, keep your seatbelt on, and watch the speed signs on the Ring Road, Canal Road and the motorway links. On the M-2 and other motorways, the Motorway Police issue tickets on the spot, and those are paid by the driver at the time.

## Breakdowns, accidents and damage

If anything happens, stop somewhere safe and call us first. For injuries call Rescue 1122, for the police call 15, and on a motorway the Motorway Police helpline is 130. Take photos of the scene and the other vehicle. Please do not let a roadside workshop repair the car: we arrange the repair, and the agreement explains how damage is assessed and what comes from the deposit.

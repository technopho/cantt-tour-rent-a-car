## When a luxury car is the right call

- **Visiting executives and board members.** Airport pickup at LHE, a hotel on The Mall or in Gulberg, meetings in the day and dinner in the evening, all in one car with one chauffeur.
- **Foreign delegations and official visitors.** Several cars moving together, with timings that cannot slip.
- **Weddings.** The couple's car for the baraat and the rukhsati, often with decoration. See [wedding car rental](/wedding-car-rental-lahore/).
- **Launches, shoots and brand events.** A car that looks right on camera, and a driver who is patient with the schedule.
- **Family occasions.** Welcoming parents home from abroad, a milestone birthday, or simply a comfortable SUV for elders on a long day.

## Which car for which occasion

Luxury bookings in Lahore usually come down to three choices.

- **A full size SUV in the Toyota Prado or Land Cruiser class.** The default for protocol movement: high seating, room for a security officer up front, a big boot for luggage and real presence at a hotel porch. Also the most comfortable choice for a VIP's day trip to Islamabad on the M-2.
- **An executive sedan in the Mercedes S-Class style.** For a single senior guest who wants a quiet, low, refined car, or for the couple's entrance at a wedding.
- **A matched set.** For a delegation, two or three SUVs of the same colour, with a Hiace van behind for support staff and bags, so the whole party moves together.

The exact cars available on your date and their current rates are in the grid and the table on this page.

## Protocol movement in Lahore

Moving important guests through Lahore is mostly planning. Roads around The Mall, Canal Road and the airport can close at short notice for official movement, and the porches at hotels and venues get crowded at peak times. Before an event we check the drop points and parking at venues such as the Alhamra Arts Council, Lahore Gymkhana, the Expo Centre or a hotel on The Mall, and agree a primary and a backup route with your team.

On the day, cars move in a set order with the lead car carrying the most senior guest or the coordinator, depending on your protocol. One coordinator stays on WhatsApp with your protocol officer and every driver. If timings change, every car knows within a minute.

## The chauffeur

Luxury bookings get our senior drivers: experienced, calm in traffic and familiar with hotel porches, embassy style gates and the etiquette of waiting. They wear formal dress, keep the car cool and ready, do not take calls while driving, and stay with the car while your guest is inside. If your guest prefers silence, they get silence.

## Presentation and condition

Every luxury car is washed and detailed before the booking: paint, glass, wheels, carpets and seats. We check tyres, brakes, lights and the air conditioning, which matters in a Lahore June. For multi day bookings the car is cleaned each morning before pickup.

## How to brief us

Send the guest names, flight numbers if any, venues and timings, the dress code for the chauffeur and any special requests. For corporate visits, a company letter or email is enough to set up billing, and our [corporate car rental](/corporate-car-rental-lahore/) account can carry the cost into your monthly invoice. For a single day, a CNIC and a WhatsApp message are all we need to begin.

## Arriving at Allama Iqbal International Airport

LHE sits on the eastern side of Lahore, next to Lahore Cantt and a short drive from DHA. After landing, international passengers go through immigration, baggage claim and customs. On a quiet afternoon that can take 30 minutes. When several Gulf flights land close together late at night, it can take an hour or more. That is why our free waiting starts at the actual landing time.

When you come out of the arrivals hall, look for your name on the board. The driver also messages you on WhatsApp, and the airport Wi-Fi is enough for this if your Pakistani SIM is not active yet.

## Late night and early morning flights

Many flights from Dubai, Doha, Jeddah, Riyadh and Europe land in Lahore between midnight and early morning. These pickups are booked in advance, and the driver is at the airport before you land. If your flight changes, send the new flight number and we update the pickup.

For departures, there is a security check on the way into the airport, and entry to the departures hall is for passengers with tickets only. Leave enough time for both, especially at peak travel times around Eid and the summer holidays.

## Families, luggage and elders

Families returning from the Gulf, the UK or the US often travel with four to six large suitcases plus cabin bags and gifts. A sedan will not take all of that. For four people with heavy luggage, choose a car with enough large bags in the rate table below, or a Hiace van for bigger families. For a family that wants to travel together and keep the luggage separate, we can send two cars.

If an elderly parent needs a wheelchair, request it from your airline before you fly; the driver meets you at the exit and helps with the transfer into the car. If you need a child seat, tell us when you book and we confirm what is available.

## Airport drop and pick in one booking

Seeing someone off and collecting someone else a few hours later? Book the car for the day instead of two trips. The day rate in the table covers the full duty hours, and the driver can wait at the airport or take you home in between.

Landing in Lahore but heading to Sialkot, Gujranwala, Faisalabad or Islamabad? The driver can take you straight from the arrivals exit to your door in that city, priced at the outstation rate. See [outstation routes](/routes/) for drive times.

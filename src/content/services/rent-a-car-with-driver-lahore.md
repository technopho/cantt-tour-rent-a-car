## Who books a car with a driver in Lahore

- **Families with a full day.** A school drop, an appointment at Services Hospital or CMH, a visit to relatives in Model Town, then dinner on MM Alam Road. One car and one driver, and nobody has to look for parking.
- **Overseas Pakistanis back home.** You land at Allama Iqbal International Airport after a few years away and the city has changed: new underpasses, the Ring Road, one-way loops in Gulberg. A driver who uses these roads every day saves you hours.
- **Business visitors.** Meetings on Main Boulevard Gulberg, a factory visit on Ferozepur Road or at Sundar Industrial Estate, then a client dinner. The driver waits outside while you work.
- **Parents and grandparents who do not drive.** Door to door, help at the gate, and patient waiting at the bank or the doctor.

## How a booking works

1. **Share the plan.** Send the date, pickup time, pickup address and roughly where you need to go. A Google Maps pin is perfect.
2. **Get a clear quote.** We reply on WhatsApp with the daily rate for the car you picked, what is included, and how fuel, tolls and overtime work.
3. **Driver details before the day.** You receive the driver's name and mobile number and the car's registration number.
4. **Pickup.** The driver arrives a few minutes early and messages you from outside. In gated societies like DHA, Askari or Bahria Town, share the gate and block number so security lets him in without a call to you.
5. **Pay as agreed.** Longer bookings usually need an advance. Payment options are listed in our [FAQ](/faq/).

## The city day, explained

A day booking covers a fixed number of duty hours, shown at the top of this page and in your quote. The clock starts at the pickup time in your booking, not when you get into the car. Waiting at your stops is included: the driver can wait at Packages Mall, outside a wedding hall or at an office in Gulberg for as long as you need within the day.

After the duty hours, overtime is billed per hour at the rate listed for your car. If you know the day will run late, for example a mehndi that ends after midnight, tell us when you book and we quote the whole day up front.

## Lahore traffic, handled

Lahore traffic has a rhythm. Canal Road, Ferozepur Road and The Mall slow down between about 8 and 10 am and again from 5 to 8 pm. Jail Road and Main Boulevard Gulberg are busy most evenings, and the roads around Liberty Market, Anarkali and Ichhra crawl on weekends. Our drivers plan around these hours, use the Ring Road and the signal free corridors where they help, and know where to park near Hall Road, Shah Alam Market and the old city.

Safe City cameras watch many junctions in Lahore. Our drivers keep to lanes and speed limits, and any challan caused by the driver is our responsibility, not yours.

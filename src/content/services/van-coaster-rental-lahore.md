## Van or coaster?

**A Hiace van** suits families, airport groups, small tours and anywhere the road gets narrow. It parks easily at homes in older neighbourhoods, fits through the lanes of a hill station and is quicker on mountain roads.

**A Coaster** suits big groups: school trips, office picnics, a baraat with forty guests, or a university tour to the north. Everyone travels together, which makes headcounts and timings much easier.

**Two vans instead of one coaster** is worth considering when a group will split up during the day, when luggage is heavy, or when part of the route is too narrow for a coaster. Send us the passenger count, the luggage and the route, and we suggest the right mix with prices.

## Northern tours from Lahore

Most northern tours start on the M-2 to Islamabad. From there:

- **Naran and Kaghan:** the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot and up the Kaghan Valley. About 12 to 14 hours from Lahore by coaster with stops. Many groups break the journey in Islamabad or Abbottabad.
- **Hunza:** the Karakoram Highway through Besham and Chilas to Gilgit and Karimabad. A two day drive each way, with a night stop on the way.
- **Skardu:** the Karakoram Highway to the Skardu road junction near Jaglot, then along the Indus. Also two days each way, and better suited to Hiace vans.
- **Murree and the Galiyat:** a long day trip, or better, a night or two. Coasters manage the main road to Murree; some upper roads suit vans better.

The Babusar Pass between Naran and Chilas usually opens in early summer and closes with the first heavy snow in autumn. We plan routes around its status and around monsoon landslides in July and August. Our route guides for [Naran and Kaghan](/routes/lahore-to-naran-kaghan/), [Hunza](/routes/lahore-to-hunza/) and [Skardu](/routes/lahore-to-skardu/) have more detail.

## Safety on long tours

Long tours are planned so the driver is rested and the mountain sections are driven in daylight. That means early starts from Lahore, proper stops at motorway service areas, and night halts on long routes rather than one exhausting push. We ask groups to keep to the agreed timings and not to pressure the driver to make up time on mountain roads. On the M-2, the Motorway Police helpline is 130.

Factories and offices use our vans and coasters for daily pick and drop on fixed routes, for example from Township and Johar Town to Kot Lakhpat, or across the Ring Road to DHA. Schools book us for educational trips to the Lahore Museum, the Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort, or a day out at Jallo Park.

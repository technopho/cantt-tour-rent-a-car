## Planning wedding transport in Lahore

Start with a simple list: who needs to be where, and when. The dulha and his closest family, the elders who should not wait outside, guests arriving from abroad and staying at hotels near the airport or in Gulberg, and the bride's family if you are arranging cars for both sides. Send us that list with the venue for each function, and we turn it into a pickup plan with times, cars and drivers.

### Mehndi and dholki

Usually evening functions at home, in a marquee or at a farmhouse on Bedian Road or Barki Road. The cars you need here are guest cars and a van or two for family groups, booked into the night. Farmhouse roads can be dark and narrow, so we share the exact pin with every driver and keep them in a WhatsApp group with your coordinator.

### Baraat

The baraat is where timing matters most. Marriage halls in Lahore, on Canal Road, Raiwind Road, in DHA, Johar Town and along the Ring Road, run strict time slots. We work back from the hall's start time: when the decorated car must leave the groom's house, when guest cars must be at the meeting point, and how long the drive takes at that hour on that day.

On the day, the couple's car leads and the guest cars follow in an agreed order. Our drivers keep the convoy together through signals without blocking roads or driving dangerously, and they do not take part in firing or stunts. Safe City cameras record convoys that jump signals, and the challans that follow are no way to start a marriage.

### Rukhsati and walima

After the rukhsati, the couple's car is waiting at the hall door with the decoration intact for the drive home. For the walima, book the same car and chauffeur for continuity, or choose something different for the reception.

## Choosing the couple's car

- **A luxury sedan** for a classic, elegant entry.
- **A large SUV** in the Prado or Land Cruiser class for a bold look and room for the bride's dress.
- **A well-kept white sedan** for a simpler wedding or as matching cars for close family.

Our [luxury car rental](/luxury-car-rental-lahore/) page lists the premium range with live rates. For guests, a run of matching sedans looks smart in photos, and a Hiace or coaster is the most practical way to move a large family. See [van and coaster rental](/van-coaster-rental-lahore/).

## Decoration, done properly

Decoration is optional and arranged with an experienced car decorator. Choose fresh roses and gajray, artificial flowers, or ribbons only. It is done an hour or two before pickup, attached with ties and suction mounts rather than tape on the paint, and removed after the function. We agree the design and its price with you in advance, so there is nothing to negotiate on the day.

## Budgeting for wedding cars

Each car is priced per function day with a chauffeur, using the rates in the table on this page. Add overtime if a function runs past the duty hours, decoration for the couple's car, and fuel if it is not included in your quote. For several functions over two or three days, ask for a package price: it is simpler to approve and usually works out better than booking each day separately.

Book early, share the list, and let us handle the cars. You have enough to think about.
